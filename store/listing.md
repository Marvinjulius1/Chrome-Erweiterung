# Chrome Web Store listing – Zenith

Copy these texts into the Chrome Web Store Developer Dashboard.

## Store listing

**Name**
Zenith

**Summary** (max. 132 characters)
A calm, minimal new tab: a beautiful photo for every time of day, the time, a greeting and a thought for the day.

**Category**
Productivity (alternative: Lifestyle)

**Language**
English

**Description**

Zenith turns every new tab into a quiet moment.

A full-screen photo that matches the time of day, a clean clock, a greeting
with your name and one thought for the day. Nothing else, unless you ask
for it.

PHOTOS FOR EVERY TIME OF DAY
• Misty sunrises at dawn, mountains and forests in the morning, open
  landscapes by day, warm sunsets at golden hour, blue hour at dusk and the
  Milky Way at night
• One calm photo per time of day, no restless switching on every tab
• See where each photo was taken, bottom left
• Save favorites, pin a photo, or skip to the next one
• Works offline once photos are cached

A CLOCK YOU LIKE
• Six styles: digital, digital with seconds, analog, flip, words
  ("Quarter past three") and bold stacked
• Above, below or beside the greeting
• 12- or 24-hour, seconds and date
• Any time zone in the world

THOUGHT OF THE DAY
After a few seconds the greeting fades into the day's thought: calm,
stoic, disciplined lines from Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Laozi and more.
Press Space to switch back and forth.

HIDDEN UNTIL YOU NEED IT
Everything else lives behind one small button:
• Focus mode: a Pomodoro timer with a progress ring, a chime and a
  notification at the end, sessions counted per day
• Daily intention: "What's your main focus today?"
• Up to five tasks for today
• Quick notes
• Quick links to your favorite sites
• Ambient sounds: rain, forest, ocean, wind
• One-minute breathing exercise
• Zen mode: only the photo and the clock

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
Space, F (focus), N (new photo), Z (zen), B (breathe), S (settings), ? (help)

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads. Your data stays in your
browser. Photos by talented photographers on Unsplash, credited in the app.

## Privacy practices tab

**Single purpose**
Zenith replaces the new tab page with a calm, minimal start screen: a
background photo for the time of day, a clock, a personal greeting and a
daily quote, plus optional focus tools.

**Permission justifications**

- `storage`: Saves the user's name, settings, tasks, notes, quick links and
  favorites in chrome.storage so they persist between new tabs.
- `alarms`: Ends a running focus (Pomodoro) session at the right time, even
  if no new tab is open at that moment.
- `notifications`: Notifies the user when a focus session or a break has
  ended.

**Remote code**
No, I am not using remote code. All JavaScript is packaged with the
extension.

**Data usage**
Select none of the data categories (the extension does not collect or
transmit user data). Then confirm the three statements:
- I do not sell or transfer user data to third parties
- I do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the single purpose
- I do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes
